# 🚀 BioNaut - NASA Space Biology Research Engine

An intelligent research platform for exploring and analyzing NASA's space biology database, powered by advanced AI and featuring interactive 3D visualizations.

## 🌟 About

BioNaut is a specialized research engine designed to help scientists, researchers, students, and space enthusiasts discover and analyze space biology research from NASA's extensive database. The platform leverages artificial intelligence to provide semantic search, intelligent recommendations, and comprehensive trend analysis.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔍 Advanced Search
- **Semantic Search**: AI-powered natural language understanding
- **Smart Filtering**: Filter by source type, year range, and research categories
- **Real-time Results**: Instant search with relevance scoring
- **Query Suggestions**: Intelligent search recommendations

### 🤖 AI-Powered Analysis
- **Intelligent Summaries**: Automated research paper summarization using Google Gemini AI
- **Deep Insights**: AI-generated interpretations and key findings
- **Connection Discovery**: Identify relationships between studies
- **Pattern Recognition**: Automated analysis across publications

### 📊 Research Insights
- **Trend Analysis**: Discover emerging topics and research patterns
- **Interactive Visualizations**: Beautiful charts and data representations
- **Statistical Metrics**: Key performance indicators and research statistics
- **Temporal Analysis**: Track research evolution over time

### 💡 Smart Recommendations
- **Personalized Suggestions**: Tailored research recommendations
- **Related Studies**: Discover similar experiments and publications
- **Research Gap Identification**: Find unexplored areas
- **Topic-based Exploration**: Browse by research themes

### 📚 Interactive 3D Library
- **3D Visualization**: Explore research in an immersive 3D environment
- **Intuitive Navigation**: Browse research by categories and topics
- **Visual Organization**: Research organized by biology, missions, and themes

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **React 18** - Modern component-based architecture
- **Vite** - Lightning-fast build tool and dev server
- **Tailwind CSS** - Utility-first CSS framework
- **Framer Motion** - Smooth animations and transitions
- **Three.js** - Powerful 3D visualization engine
- **Recharts** - Beautiful, responsive charts
- **Google Gemini AI** - Advanced AI analysis
- **Axios** - HTTP client for API communication

## 📖 Usage Guide

### Search Interface

Enter research topics or questions in natural language:
- "microgravity effects on bone density"
- "plant growth experiments on ISS"
- "immune system response in astronauts"
- "space radiation effects on DNA"
- "muscle atrophy in long-duration spaceflight"

### AI Analysis

1. Search for research papers
2. Click "Generate Summary" on any result
3. Get instant AI-powered insights and analysis
4. Explore related research recommendations

### 3D Library

- Navigate through the interactive 3D space
- Click on categories to explore research areas
- Use mouse/trackpad to rotate and zoom
- Filter by different research themes
